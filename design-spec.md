# Soft WAV design direction

## Core idea
Soft WAV should feel like a premium, intimate music product: calm, warm, editorial, and emotionally intelligent. It should borrow the discipline and polish of Apple product design without copying its aesthetic or tone. The result should feel like a luxury listening ritual, not a generic app shell.

## Product feeling
- quiet confidence
- premium but not cold
- emotional and human
- minimal, polished, intentional
- late-night, warm, reflective
- editorial and curated rather than crowded or utilitarian

## Visual principles
- generous spacing and clean structure
- strong visual hierarchy with restrained color usage
- dark surfaces with warm neutrals and softened highlights
- subtle gold / champagne accents rather than loud saturation
- gentle gradients and low-glare contrast
- minimal chrome; no noisy UI elements
- soft borders and shadows, not harsh outlines

## Color palette
### Base
- Background: #080809
- Surface: #111113
- Panel: #0d0d0f
- Text: #e9e5df
- Muted text: #8b8580
- Border: rgba(233,229,223,0.07)

### Accent
- Gold: #c9a97f
- Gold hover: #f0ce98
- Soft warm ivory: #f3efe9

### supporting accent tones
- Blue: #7fa0b8
- Purple: #a08ec7
- Green: #7cb8a0
- Red: #c47c7c

## Typography
- Headings: Cormorant Garamond, elegant serif
- UI / labels: DM Mono, technical but restrained

## Layout rules
- large breathing room around major sections
- preview cards should feel like objects in a gallery
- card content should be cleanly stacked with strong rhythm
- no dense text blocks or visual overload
- keep interface operations simple and tactile

## Component language
### Buttons
- low-noise, subtle borders
- muted surfaces by default
- gold action buttons for primary actions only
- soft hover transitions
- avoid heavy shadows or aggressive contrast

### Cards
- dark, soft-edged containers
- fine borders
- album art and content aligned in a quiet editorial way
- inner spacing balanced and spacious

### Search / input
- dark field with subtle border
- warm neutral text
- focus state must be clean and gold-tinted, not flashy

### Playlist / result surfaces
- feel curated and intentional
- not utility-heavy
- read like a recommendation object rather than a dashboard widget

## Motion
- slow fade-ins for content blocks
- gentle easing curves
- minimal animation intensity
- transitions should feel premium and calm

## Tone and copy
- poetic but controlled
- intimate, tasteful, descriptive
- never loud or playful in a gimmicky way
- copy should feel like an editorial note, not a chatbot dump

## Brand essence
Soft WAV is not trying to mimic Apple. It is taking the same product discipline — clarity, restraint, polish — and layering it with emotional music culture and soulful curation.

The final feeling should be:
“quiet luxury meets soulful listening.”
